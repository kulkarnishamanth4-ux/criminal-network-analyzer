Details of the Offence:
This FIR is being registered on the basis of a reliable source information and subsequent preliminary inquiry conducted by SI Arvind Mehta into a large-scale financial fraud, fake invoicing, and money laundering syndicate operating primarily within the jurisdictions of Chandni Chowk, Paharganj, and Karol Bagh. 

According to the received intelligence, Vikram Tiwari urf Vicky has been operating a network of shell companies under the guise of legitimate import-export businesses. During our surveillance, it was noted that a non-existent firm named Bharat Trading Co. was registered using forged documents. The primary accused, Vikram Tiwari, along with Suresh Pandey, has been manufacturing forged transport receipts and fake GST invoices to claim input tax credits illegally. 

On 10-03-2024, suspicious movements of cash were observed. An amount of Rs. 15,00,000 was reportedly transferred by Rakesh Sharma to Nafisa Begum alias Nafi for facilitating the hawala transactions overseas. Rakesh Sharma was seen arriving at the Om Enterprises office in Karol Bagh using a white SUV bearing registration number DL-05-CQ-4521. This vehicle is registered under a fictitious name but has been continually used by the syndicate for moving unaccounted cash between hawala operators in the city. 

Further investigation revealed that Suresh Pandey handles the logistics and documentation for the syndicate. He was intercepted earlier this month near Paharganj in a sedan with the license plate UP-32-AB-7890, from which several blank letterheads of Bharat Trading Co. and Om Enterprises were recovered. 

Call detail records (CDR) analysis indicates frequent communication between the accused. The primary contact numbers being used to orchestrate these activities include +91-98765-43210 (registered to Vikram Tiwari), +91-91234-56789 (used by Nafisa Begum), and +91-99887-76655 (used by Suresh Pandey). These individuals have been coordinating the movement of illicit funds and forged documents over secured messaging apps and regular voice calls, plotting to defraud the exchequer of several crores of rupees. 

This clearly establishes a well-organized criminal conspiracy under Section 120B of the IPC. The creation of fake firms like Bharat Trading Co., generating bogus invoices, and laundering the proceeds of crime satisfy the ingredients of cheating (Sec 420 IPC), forgery of valuable security (Sec 467 IPC), forgery for purpose of cheating (Sec 468 IPC), and using forged documents as genuine (Sec 471 IPC). 

Keeping in view the gravity of the offences and the large-scale conspiracy, this FIR is being lodged for deep and thorough investigation. Investigation of this case is handed over to Inspector Rajinder Singh, EOW.
